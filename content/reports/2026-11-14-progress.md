## 3. What we learned

1. **Reach is fine, action is zero.** People watch but nobody likes, comments or taps through to the profile.
2. **MoveMRR ran 47.6 s**, over the 25 to 40 s spec. Viewers who leave early never reach the twist or the question.
3. **Mixed currencies confuse** (€0.04 → $300 on the first frame). One currency per reel.
4. **Hook text must be fully readable on frame 1.** The MoveMRR hook line started faded.
5. **Big company stories underperform** with this audience (ChatGPT 1.2B: 23 views). Small software businesses fit better.
6. **The story has to say its point out loud.** Postiz v1 read like an ad; v2 states the lesson ("free code, paid convenience").
7. India share comes from Instagram testing near the account's location first. It should fall as US and EU accounts engage.
