## What is working right now (from the viral research skill)

Corpus: 43 YouTube Shorts from Starter Story (901K subscribers, account median about 11K views), ranked by how far each beat that median. One account only, so treat this as direction, not proof.

| Outlier | Views | Title |
|---|---|---|
| 25x | 277K | Niche $40K/month micro saas |
| 10x | 111K | Bro built a $1.3M business in 6 7 hours |
| 8x | 89K | This guy built a $15K/month AI app in a day |
| 7x | 81K | Taught himself to use Cursor and built a $300K AI app |
| 6.5x | 71K | He built 28 apps. They make $10K/month. Same tech stack every time. |
| 5x | 53K | Bro makes $40K per month with such a simple website |

The pattern in the top third:
1. **The money number comes first** and it is a monthly number people can picture.
2. **Simple or boring contrast**: "such a simple website", "micro saas", "nobody talks about".
3. **Time collapse**: "in a day", "in 6 hours", "in 4 weeks".
4. **Short**: 5 to 12 words.

The bottom third: tech stack lists, advice quotes and lifestyle lines ("never checks his phone before noon"). Those sat at 0.2x to 0.4x.

So every hook below leads with the number, keeps the "it is simple" contrast where it is true, and stays under 12 words.
