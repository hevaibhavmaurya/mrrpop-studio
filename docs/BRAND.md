## Scope — what we cover (and what we don't)

Cover **tech software products only**:
- Products making revenue (MRR/ARR milestones)
- "Made $X in Y days" stories
- New launches worth knowing
- How a builder grew from $0 → $X
- Paid showcases and sponsorships (always labelled)
- Acquisitions & exits (Deal Pop series)

Do NOT cover: gadgets, phones, crypto prices, general tech gossip, AI model benchmark wars, layoffs news.

## Trust rules (non-negotiable) — evidence label on every number

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| ✓ **Payment-verified** | Independently checked via payment-provider data (Stripe, TrustMRR, etc.). Never from an interview quote. |
| **Company-announced** | Confirmed in an official company announcement or filing. |
| **Founder-reported** | The founder publicly stated it. Attributed, not verified. |
| **Estimated** | Inferred from indirect signals. Avoid precise figures; prefer not to publish. |
| **Sponsored** | Paid placement. Labelled on screen and in caption. |

Every Reel carries a source line at the bottom: "Source: <who/what> · <date>" next to its label.
Never invent numbers, dates or quotes. Payment never buys a label or editorial wording.
Never reuse other creators' footage. Product screenshots: from the product's own site/press kit, credited.

## Voice and tone

- Short sentences. One idea per line. Numbers first.
- Curious and excited, never hype-y or fake. "That's a pop." is our catchphrase for a big number.
- Say numbers the way people speak: "twelve thousand a month", not "12K MRR" (on screen we write "$12K MRR").
- End most Reels with a question to drive comments ("Would you build this?").
- Sign-off line: "Follow MRR Pop — the business behind software."

Words we like: builder, shipped, launched, made, paying customers, solo.
Words we avoid: guru, passive income, get rich, insane (overused), secret, hack.
