## Goal
Reach founders, indie hackers and SaaS fans in the **US, UK and Europe** (Germany, Netherlands).
Followers only count if they watch reels. Quality of audience beats follower count.

## Hard rules
- Never publish, post, comment or DM on the user's behalf. Draft it; Vaibhav posts by hand.
- Comments and replies are written or checked by Vaibhav. No automated or bulk AI replies (spam flags on new accounts, and it reads as a bot).
- Never touch passwords, 2FA, email or phone settings.
- Plain, short, true wording. No hyphens, dashes or ellipses, no "Boom". "Humanized" rewrites were judged worse; don't add filler.
- A reply from a founder is a lead, not a fact. Nothing gets featured until the number is sourced (STORY-PROTOCOL.md).

## What works on X for a new account
Learned from Ifty Mashruf's "0 to 1,000 followers in 2 days" article (x.com/Mashruf32/status/2121907346343690614, Nov 15 2026), adapted for a brand account:
1. **Conversations beat content at first.** Replies to founder posts are the main growth engine.
2. **Reply to every reply and ask one real question back** ("How did you get your first 10 customers?"). Never just "Thanks!"
3. **Invitation posts, but with a purpose.** Not generic "let's connect" (attracts follow for follow accounts that churn and never watch reels). Use:
   - "Building a SaaS? Drop it below with your MRR. Mr. Pop is picking stories for next week's reels. Small apps welcome, $500 MRR counts. Verified revenue (Stripe, TrustMRR) gets looked at first."
   - "Small SaaS founders, don't hide. What did your app make last month? One line, no links needed."
4. **Use each invitation format 2 or 3 times max.** It decays fast (259 → 104 → 112 → 13 replies in the article).
5. **Tips and threads from a new account get read, not answered.** End every post with an easy question.
6. **Space posts out.** Bursts (10 posts in 90 min) reach almost nobody.
7. **Bio a stranger gets in 5 seconds.** Ours already does.
8. **One main post a day while the account is small.** Don't post an invitation the same day as a story post; give each its own 7:30 to 8 pm IST slot. Only post an invitation when Vaibhav can answer replies for the next hour, and actually feature someone from the replies (the post makes a promise).
9. **Skip:** daily follower count posts from the brand (fine from Vaibhav's personal account tagging @getmrrpop), replying in non English languages (pulls reach away from US/EU), and high volume AI replies.

## What works on Instagram so far (week 1)
- Reach is fine, actions are zero. Every reel needs a question that is easy to answer and a reason to tap the profile.
- Keep reels 25 to 35 s. One currency per reel. Hook fully readable on frame 1.
- Small software businesses beat big company stories with this audience.
- The reel must say its point out loud (Postiz v1 read like an ad; v2 says "free code, paid convenience").
- Carousels: 1 to 2 a week on non reel days, 1080×1350, built with `/ig-carousel`. Best for lists and comparisons ("3 apps under $10K MRR"). Same fact rules as reels: evidence label and source on the slide, sources in the caption.
- Instagram web can't reset suggested content, pin reels, edit links or show countries. Those are app only.
